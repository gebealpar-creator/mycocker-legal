MyCocker Privacy Policy

Effective: October 7, 2026

In short: MyCocker has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. We, the developer, cannot see your data.

Who we are

MyCocker is an iOS app for Cocker Spaniel owners, developed by Gebe Alpar (“we”). You can reach us at gebe.alpar@gmail.com.

What the app stores

The information you choose to enter, for example:

  • your dogs’ profiles (name, photo, sex, birth date, microchip, weight, coat colour);
  • appointments, reminders and habits (vaccines, deworming, vet visits, walks, meals);
  • health journal entries, photos and documents you add;
  • your owner profile (name and photo), contacts you save (such as your vet) and products in Quick Shop.

This data is stored on your device. We do not collect it, and it is never sent to us.

iCloud sync and sharing

If you are signed in to iCloud, MyCocker syncs your data through Apple’s CloudKit to your private iCloud database, so it is available on your other devices and after reinstalling. This storage belongs to your Apple account; we have no access to it. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

If you share a dog with someone, that dog’s data (its profile, appointments, journal, weight and documents) becomes visible to the people you invite through iCloud. You can stop sharing at any time from the dog’s profile.

Permissions

  • Photos and camera – only when you add a photo or scan a document. Only the pictures you pick are used.
  • Notifications – reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push marketing.
  • Calendar – only if you tap “Add to iPhone Calendar”, to create that one event.

MyCocker does not use your location in this version.

Connections to other services

  • Product links – when you add a link in Quick Shop, the app opens that web page from your device to read the product’s name, picture and price. The store’s website sees a normal page visit, as in a browser.
  • Calls and WhatsApp – tapping “Call” or “WhatsApp” on a contact opens the Phone app or WhatsApp.
  • Community groups – links open in your browser or the group’s app.
  • Purchases – Premium is bought through Apple’s App Store. We never receive your payment details.

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Backups

When you export a backup (a .mycocker file), it is saved wherever you choose (for example Files or AirDrop). Keep it somewhere safe: it contains your dogs’ data and photos.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a dog from its profile. You can remove it from this iPhone only, or from iCloud too.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on the device.
  • Data in iCloud can also be removed in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → MyCocker.

Children

MyCocker is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
